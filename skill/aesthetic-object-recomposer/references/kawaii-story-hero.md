# Kawaii Story Hero

Use this reference when the user asks for an expressive kawaii story image, a social-post hero image, or a result with the energetic look of the supplied examples. This is a **distinct route** from the light caption-only story mode. Keep the approved Kawaii Clean photograph as the immutable base, but allow a deliberately bolder storytelling overlay.

## Route selection

Use **Hero Story** by default for food, drinks, casual desktop scenes, and group meals when the user asks for lively, cute, playful, sticker-like, social-ready, or story-forward results. Use **Light Story** only when the user explicitly asks for minimal, premium, restrained, or unobtrusive annotations.

| Dimension | Hero Story | Light Story |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Dominant phrase | One large 4–8-character Chinese exclamation | One small 4–12-character caption |
| Captions | One hero phrase plus 1–3 supporting reactions | One to three small captions |
| Bubble style | One large, transparent irregular white-outline cloud is allowed | Transparent outline only; keep small or omit |
| Overlay coverage | 10–18% of canvas; never more than 22% | 3–8% of canvas |
| Accent palette | White linework plus 1–2 source-derived warm accents | Primarily white, sparse accents |
| Decorative rhythm | 6–14 small marks, distributed around the frame | 2–6 small marks |
| Character acting | Explicit, expressive and physically attached | Simple, secondary emotion |

## Composition recipe

1. Reserve 20–35% of the cleanest negative space for **one dominant headline**, preferably in the upper third. Set it at roughly 20–30% of the canvas width. Use bold, rounded white hand lettering with a dark brown or charcoal keyline and a soft light shadow. Do not use regular typeset fonts.
2. Put the headline inside one transparent, irregular white-outline speech cloud only when the background needs separation. Make the cloud follow the letter silhouettes closely; it must not read as a rigid sticker or UI card.
3. Assign a role to each selected object. The hero shows the clearest face and action. One support object may react. Avoid drawing faces on every object in a dense spread.
4. Add one to three short supporting phrases, each 3–7 Chinese characters. Place them in readable negative space and connect them to the speaking object with a hand-drawn curved arrow, short burst lines, or a small outlined mini-bubble.
5. Use a perimeter rhythm of small marks: stars, hearts, music notes, lightning, steam curls, focus rays, blush marks, sweat drops, tiny flags, and motion strokes. Cluster them around objects and headline edges; do not scatter them evenly.
6. Use white as the primary outline. Add only one or two accent colors sampled from the scene, such as yolk yellow, tomato red, orange drink amber, muted coral, or leafy green. Use accents on punctuation and small decorations, not as caption backgrounds.
7. Preserve the source photo and Kawaii Clean illustration exactly: do not move objects, change food, alter faces that already exist, regenerate labels, or modify lighting/crop/focus. The overlay should sit convincingly on top of the existing photograph.

## Prompt insert — Hero Story third pass

> Use the supplied approved Kawaii Clean image as an immutable base. Preserve every photographic pixel and all existing character faces, limbs, props, and symbols. Transform only the story overlay into a high-energy kawaii social-post hero composition. Reserve clean negative space in the upper third for one large exact Chinese headline: [HERO HEADLINE]. Render it in bold rounded white hand-drawn lettering with a slim dark brown keyline and soft light shadow. Surround it with one transparent irregular white-outline cloud only if needed for contrast; the cloud must be organic, close to the lettering, and never a filled card. Add [SUPPORTING CAPTION 1] near [ANCHOR 1] and [SUPPORTING CAPTION 2] near [ANCHOR 2], each in smaller white handwritten lettering with a fine keyline and a curved pointer. Add [MARKS] as a deliberate asymmetric perimeter rhythm. Use only white plus [ACCENT COLOR 1] and [ACCENT COLOR 2] sampled from the photo. Keep total added overlay around 10–18% of the canvas, keeping labels, food texture, faces, hands, controls, hero silhouette, and important highlights unobstructed. Do not move, resize, replace, remove, duplicate, or restyle any photographed object or existing kawaii character. No cream cards, colored fill boxes, pill badges, social-media UI, generic fonts, dense paragraphs, fake labels, or full-scene cartoon conversion.

## Hero Story quality gate

Accept only when the large phrase is readable at thumbnail size, the headline has a clear hierarchy over the smaller reactions, decorations form intentional clusters rather than random noise, the photo still reads as the base scene, and the composition contains no object or image drift outside added overlay pixels. Reduce marks before reducing headline energy.
