# Visual Language

## Default mood

Create a quiet independent-café or design-magazine photograph: warm, tactile, lived-in, unforced, and aspirational without looking like an advertisement.

## Environment

- Prefer walnut, teak, oak, linen, matte plaster, aged leather, ceramic, clear glass, and printed paper.
- Use a window, soft textured wall, wooden chair, round side table, or open book as structural anchors when a scene rebuild is allowed.
- Keep the set sparse. One table surface and one background story cue are usually enough.
- Avoid luxury marble, neon signage, excessive flowers, fairy lights, and generic stock-photo props unless present in the source.

## Light

- Use one directional window light source from the side or rear three-quarter angle.
- Prefer late-morning sun or restrained golden-hour warmth.
- Allow geometric patches of sunlight and long soft-edged shadows.
- Preserve highlight texture; avoid clipped white plates, glowing glass rims, and crushed black furniture.
- Use gentle negative fill for shape, not high-contrast commercial lighting.

## Camera

- Default to a natural 35–50 mm full-frame equivalent.
- Use 45–70° three-quarter tabletop views for grouped objects.
- Use 70–90° overhead views when arrangement geometry is the main story.
- Use eye-level or slightly low views for a single product or camera.
- Use f/2.8–f/5.6 equivalent depth: hero sharp, support readable, background gradually soft.
- Keep vertical architecture credible and avoid extreme wide-angle distortion.

## Color and finish

- Build around warm brown, cream, muted olive, soft gray, and the source object's accent color.
- Keep saturation restrained and skin/food colors natural.
- Use gentle filmic contrast, fine grain, soft highlight roll-off, and slightly lifted shadows.
- Avoid orange color casts, teal-orange grading, heavy bloom, HDR clarity, and fake vintage damage.

## Output character

- Photographic realism first; styling second.
- Editorial lifestyle rather than catalog isolation.
- Imperfect but intentional placement.
- No text overlay, layout diagram, border, watermark, or social interface unless requested.
